## The Problem

<Callout type="warn" title="Losing track of everyday spending">
  Young adults often overlook small daily expenses and struggle to organize
  spending across different events like trips, outings, and celebrations —
  making it difficult to understand where their money goes.
</Callout>

## The Challenge

- Encourage young adults to build a **consistent expense-tracking habit**.
- **Reduce the effort** required to log expenses, making tracking quick and intuitive.

## My Role

As the Product Designer, I was responsible for understanding user needs through
research, designing intuitive user experiences and interfaces, creating
interactive prototypes, and ensuring the app provided a seamless, effortless
experience for tracking personal finances.

## Research Insights

Three recurring problems surfaced from research:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Small expenses go unnoticed">
    Small, frequent purchases are easy to forget, making it difficult for users
    to understand where their money is actually going.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Existing apps feel complex">
    Many expense trackers prioritize advanced features, making quick expense
    logging feel time-consuming and overwhelming.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No context for spending">
    Users struggle to separate expenses for trips, outings, or special
    occasions from everyday spending, reducing financial clarity.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Design Goals

1. **Effortless logging** — make expense entry fast enough for daily use.
2. **Context-first organization** — group expenses by trips, outings, and events.
3. **Habit building** — encourage a consistent tracking routine.
4. **Clarity at a glance** — present spending insights through a clean, intuitive interface.

## Ideation

Big ideas that emerged during the brainstorming session:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Event-Based Organization">
    Dedicated spaces for trips, outings, festivals, or any personal event —
    keeping related transactions together for better context and budgeting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Visual Spending Insights">
    Represent spending through charts and visual summaries, helping users
    quickly understand where their money goes without reading long transaction
    lists.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Financial Awareness Cards">
    Bite-sized educational tips and insights that encourage smarter financial
    habits and better money management over time.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Feature Selection

Each "How might we" question from research was mapped to a core feature:

<Accordions type="multiple">
  <Accordion title="How might we help users organize expenses for different events?">
    **Event-Based Organization** — create dedicated *Spaces* for trips,
    outings, festivals, or personal events, allowing users to keep related
    transactions together and view event-specific spending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How might we help users quickly understand their spending patterns?">
    **Visual Insights** — charts, summaries, and visual analytics help users
    scan their spending at a glance and identify trends without reading long
    transaction lists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How might we make expense logging simple enough to encourage daily use?">
    **Local-First Experience** — fast, reliable expense logging that works
    instantly, even offline, so users can record cash or manual transactions
    whenever they occur.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

### Testing Results

- Users **quickly understood the onboarding** flow.
- **Event-based organization** made expense tracking more meaningful.
- The **Home screen** provided a clear overview of finances at a glance.
- Users preferred a **minimal expense entry flow** with fewer steps.
- **Visual spending insights** helped users identify spending patterns faster.
